Responsible gaming

Gaming should be optional entertainment for adults, never a way to earn income or recover losses.

Set limits first

  • Decide a spending limit you can afford to lose.
  • Set a time limit and take regular breaks.
  • Never borrow money or use essential household funds.
  • Do not play when stressed, impaired, or trying to chase a loss.

Warning signs

Pause if gaming is affecting sleep, work, relationships, finances, or mood; if you hide activity; or if limits are repeatedly broken.

Take action

Use account limits, cooling-off periods, or self-exclusion tools offered by the service. Ask support how to close or restrict an account. Consider speaking with a qualified local counsellor or trusted person.

18+ only. If you feel at immediate risk of harming yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services now.
