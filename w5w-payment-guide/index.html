W5W Deposit and Withdrawal Guide for India

Understand commonly displayed India payment channels, account verification, transaction checks and safer payment habits before using the W5WCOM App.

UPIIMPSPaytmPhonePe

Payment methods shown for W5W users

Available methods may include UPI, UPI QR, IMPS, Paytm and PhonePe. Availability, limits, fees and processing time depend on the active provider, account status and location. Always rely on the options displayed inside your current account.

UPI and UPI QR

Confirm the recipient details and amount before approving a payment in your UPI application.

IMPS and bank transfer

Keep the transaction reference and check account history before repeating a transfer.

Paytm and PhonePe

Use the payment route displayed in the account and do not send funds to details received privately.

Account verification

Withdrawals may require identity and payment-account verification under the active platform rules.

Before making a deposit

  1. Check the minimum and maximum amount displayed for the selected method.
  2. Confirm the payment recipient and reference before approving the transaction.
  3. Keep a record of the completed payment screen and transaction ID.
  4. Do not repeat a payment immediately if the account balance has not refreshed.
Never share a UPI PIN, banking password, complete OTP or card-security code with support or another player.

Withdrawal checks and delays

Processing time can depend on account verification, provider availability and transaction review. Confirm that the receiving account belongs to you and that the submitted details match your verified profile. If a withdrawal remains pending, collect the transaction reference and contact the active support channel instead of creating another account.
