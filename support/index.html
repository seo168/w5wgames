Support and troubleshooting

APK will not install

Check Android compatibility and storage, confirm the download completed, and review the APK safety checklist. Enable unknown-source installation only for the browser used, then disable it afterward.

Account access

Use the platform's official recovery flow. Never share a password, payment PIN, seed phrase, remote-access code, or one-time password with anyone claiming to be support.

Deposit or withdrawal issue

Save the transaction reference and screenshot, confirm the recipient details, and contact the relevant service channel. Processing times, verification, and limits depend on the provider.

Contact channel

Open the external live-chat service. Its availability and response time are not guaranteed by this website.

Redact sensitive banking and identity details before sending screenshots.
